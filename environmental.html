Book Design (example section)

Tasked with creating an exhibition as a final project in the class, I chose Gyaru, a Japanese substyle I enjoy due to its decorative elements. I wanted to represent the bold colors it encapsulates while guiding the audience through its seemingly quite dark origins of grooming and retaliating against beauty standards.
