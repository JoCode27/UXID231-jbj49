Book Design (example section)

Tasked with 3 books by an author, here is a series of Murakami books. My thought process was to keep his undertones of human disturbance and post WWII trauma, yet truly mask the graininess of human nature under bright, busy "Tokyo" colors: to represent the loose mask Japanese society wears in the irony of wartime nationalism and violence to depressive consumerism and solitude.
